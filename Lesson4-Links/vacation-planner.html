Trip Research

I've been wanting to visit Hokkaido, Japan for a long time. The official tourism website (opens in a new tab) gives an area guide for tourists as well as useful information for your trip. To see how previous visitors felt about Hokkaido, I went on to their Tripadvisor page (opens in a new tab). I also wanted to know exactly what type of weather I should pack for, I made sure to check the monthly weather reports (opens in a new tab) as well.

Transportation Info

When I'm actually there, I will be able to get around quickly by using local buses and regional JR Hokkaido trains accesible through JR Hokkaido Railway Company (opens in a new tab). To navigate the unfamiliar streets and railway systems, I will use Google Maps (opens in a new tab).
