Kimchi Fried Rice

I chose to write the recipie of Kimchi Fried Rice for a variety of reasons. First of all, it is simple and quick to make. I eat this often because it is delicious but also because I can eat it when I don't have a lot of time. I also really love this meal because it is warm, comforting and filling.

  • Ingredients
  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients:

  • sesame oil
  • short-grain white rice
  • kimchi
  • egg
  • bacon bits
  • scallion

Steps:

  1. Heat a pan with sesame oil
  2. Put kimchi and bacon bits on the pan
  3. Put the rice in after waiting for a bit
  4. Fry and mix the ingredients in the pan
  5. Fry an egg separately
  6. Top fried rice with the fried egg and some scallions

Notes:

  • Equipment
    • Frying pan
    • Spatula
    • stove
  • Substitutions
    • Sausage instead of bacon bits
    • Pork belly instead of bacon bits
    • dried seaweed flakes instead of scallion
