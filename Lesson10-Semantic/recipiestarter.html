Kimchi Fried Rice

Jump to ingredients · Steps

Ingredients

  • sesame oil
  • short-grain white rice
  • kimchi
  • egg
  • bacon bits
  • scallion

Steps

  1. Heat a pan with sesame oil
  2. Put kimchi and bacon bits on the pan
  3. Put the rice in after waiting for a bit
  4. Fry and mix the ingredients in the pan
  5. Fry an egg separately
  6. Top fried rice with the fried egg and some scallions

Why this recipie:

I chose this recipie because it is very easy to cook and convient if you want to grab a quick bite.

Fun fact: Kimchi Fried Rice actually boosts your gut health because of the kimchi fermentation process that creates good bacteria!
